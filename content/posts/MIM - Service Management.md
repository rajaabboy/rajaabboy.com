## Major Incident Management: More Than Just Incident Resolution
Major Incident Management: More Than Just Incident Resolution
Can Major Incident Management (MIM) be a separate Managed Service?
Absolutely.

But can MIM operate in isolation?

I don't believe so.

In a modern Global Service Delivery organization, MIM should be the orchestration layer connecting the entire ITSM and Service Operations ecosystem.

A Major Incident may start with an application, infrastructure, platform, release or change — but its impact ultimately reaches the Business and Customer.
Therefore, MIM needs strong integration with:
🔹 Incident Management
🔹 Problem Management
🔹 Change Management
🔹 Release Management
🔹 Defect Management
🔹 Knowledge Management

And equally important, it needs strong collaboration across:
Knowledge Database | Product & Engineering | Global Delivery | Customer Support | Customer Success
For me, effective MIM comes down to a few critical success criteria:
✅ System Health Checks – Know the health of critical platforms before an incident occurs.
✅ Rapid Stakeholder Mobilization – Quickly engage the right suspected stakeholders; don't wait for organizational boundaries to slow down recovery.
✅ MTTR Optimization – Focus on restoring business service quickly while maintaining the right level of control.
✅ SRE Principles – Build reliability, observability, automation and resilience into the operating model.
✅ Business-Focused Communication – Communicate impact, actions, risks and recovery clearly to impacted business stakeholders.
✅ Post-Mortem & RCA – Don't stop when the service is restored.

Most importantly:
Close the Loop.
Incident → Recovery → RCA → Corrective Action → Knowledge → Engineering/Product Improvement → Prevention

That's when MIM moves beyond being an incident response function and becomes a true Operational Resilience & Customer Success capability.

The real measure of MIM isn't only:
"How quickly did we resolve the incident?"
It is also:
"What did we learn, what did we improve, and how did we make the platform more resilient for the next time?"

That, in my view, is the future of Intelligent Service Operations.
