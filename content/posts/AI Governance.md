## AI is Advancing
AI is Advancing. Is Our Governance Keeping Pace?

The world is racing to adopt AI. But are we moving fast enough to govern it responsibly?

Humanity has always feared threats from nature and destructive weapons. 

Today, we face a new challenge: an artificial intelligence that could evolve beyond our ability to control its consequences.

Should we wait for a crisis before establishing stronger safeguards?

It's time to rethink our priorities:

🌍 Global Cooperation — Establish stronger international AI governance.

🛡️ Governance by Design — Build safety, boundaries, and human oversight into AI from day one.

📈 Responsible ROI — Balance innovation and productivity with trust, security, and accountability.

The goal is not to slow innovation. It is to ensure innovation does not outpace our ability to govern it.

As technology leaders, we must look beyond how quickly we can adopt AI and ask how responsibly we can scale it.

Let's make AI Governance by Design a global leadership priority—not an afterthought.
